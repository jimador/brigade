## Proposal

Publish the three ready leaves nothing else waits on first: the confirmation email (T-3), the
telemetry consumer (T-7) and the no-fly polygon loader (T-8). Then publish the four leaves whose
prerequisites are now on the board: the courier parity suite (T-4), the drone dispatch queue

prerequisite's ticket into its dependent's blocked_by. T-9 and T-10 stay unpublished until the
telemetry consumer and the polygon loader land.
